How to Play

  1. Analyze the End: Read the sequence goal carefully. Slots 1 through 5 represent an exacting chronological, hierarchical, or structural progression.
  2. Select & Position: Tap an active open slot, then tap any tile in the pool to place it. Tapping an already filled slot clears it and returns it to the ice.
  3. Four End Attempts: Submitting tests your full 5-stone lineup. Correct items lock permanently in place (✓). Incorrect tiles return to the pool for recalibration.
  4. Tactician's Hint: After 2 unsuccessful attempts, an authoritative strategic clue unlocks to guide your remaining deliveries.
